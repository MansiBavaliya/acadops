# AcadOps 🤖

### Your AI Academic Operations Agent

> Students don't need another to-do list. They need an agent that understands everything competing for their attention and helps decide what deserves it next.

**AcadOps** is an adaptive AI agent designed to understand a student's academic workload, detect risks and conflicts, prioritize actions, and continuously adapt as circumstances change.

## 🎯 What Problem Does AcadOps Solve?

Students manage assignments, exams, projects, presentations, classes, and deadlines across different places. Traditional planners can store these tasks, but they don't understand the student's changing situation or help make decisions.

AcadOps turns scattered academic commitments into an intelligent, continuously updated decision system.

## 🧠 Academic Decision Intelligence

AcadOps goes beyond generating a timetable.

It can:

- 🗣️ Understand academic information through natural conversation
- 🧠 Remember relevant academic context
- 🎯 Determine the next best action
- 🚨 Detect deadline and workload risks
- 🔀 Identify conflicts between academic commitments
- ⏱️ Estimate workload against available time
- 🔄 Adapt the plan when circumstances change
- 💡 Explain why an action was prioritized
- 🔮 Evaluate "what-if" scenarios
- 📊 Track academic workload and progress
